The gap we close

Manufacturing is solved. Selling is not.

Most factories that try to enter the US market do not fail on product quality. They fail because selling in America is a separate discipline — one that runs on US consumer language, marketplace mechanics, advertising spend and buyer relationships on the ground.

How it works

Four stages, in order.

We do not launch a product before the economics are proven on paper. This sequence exists to stop money being spent on a product that cannot clear its own landed cost.

01

Product review

We look at your current catalogue and identify which products have a genuine position in the US market — and which do not.

02

Landed cost model

Tariffs, freight, marketplace fees, returns and advertising modelled before anything ships. If the margin is not there, we say so at this stage.

03

Brand & launch build

Brand, packaging, photography, listing and storefront are produced together, then the product goes live with a defined launch plan.

04

Operate & scale

Ongoing advertising, inventory and account management — with reporting back to you on sell-through, spend and margin.
